The Genesis — Where Gold Grows

Rooted in the Mist of the Western Ghats

True black pepper (Piper nigrum) is not a factory product; it is a child of ancient soil, heavy monsoon rains, and tropical altitude.

Our pepper is harvested by generational farmers nestled in the high-elevation slopes of the Western Ghats. Here, the soil is volcanic and rich in organic nutrients, nurturing vines that mature slowly under filtered canopy light. This slow maturation is nature’s way of packing maximum density, rich resin, and intense pungency into every single berry.

  1. Hand-Harvested at Peak MaturityWe never harvest green or premature spikes. Every cluster is hand-inspected and picked only when the basal berries begin to turn a rich, golden-orange hue.
  2. Traditional Sun-DryingNo accelerated chemical drying or high-heat industrial kilns. Our berries are sun-dried on clean concrete and bamboo yards for 7 to 10 days until they achieve the ideal moisture equilibrium and that signature wrinkled, leathery skin.
  3. Zero Chemical InterventionsWashed naturally in spring water, free from artificial waxes, chemical brighteners, or fumigants.
